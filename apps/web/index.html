SyncPlayer: watch videos together, in sync

SyncPlayer keeps video and audio playback aligned between friends, straight from the browser. Start a room, share the 4-digit code or invite link, and play a local file or a direct media link. Everyone sees the same moment, with no account and no media server in the middle.

What it does

  • Synchronized play, pause and seek over WebRTC peer-to-peer connections
  • Streams your local video or audio file directly to viewers
  • Plays direct media URLs, HLS (.m3u8) and MPEG-DASH (.mpd) streams
  • Host approval for new viewers, automatic drift correction and live latency stats
